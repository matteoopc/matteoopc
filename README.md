### Matteo Baitelli: iPhone apps and home automation built to be used every day

I build native apps for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and the new iPhone Duo, and I run a smart home on Home Assistant, Proxmox and a couple of NAS boxes. Most of what I make starts from a problem of my own: how much a full tank really costs, what a card collection is worth, whether a deal is actually a deal.
